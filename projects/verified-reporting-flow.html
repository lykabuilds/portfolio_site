Verified Reporting Flow

An AI analyst drafts the report. A separate checker recomputes the numbers from raw data. Finance makes the final call.

Illustrative example. All figures are invented.

1 · Raw data

Source ledger

Transactions pulled from the system each week. This is the only source of truth.

2 · Analyst agent

Drafts the summary

Writes commentary and key figures, such as sales, costs, and margin. Its output is a draft, not a result.

3 · Checker agent

Recomputes and compares

Rebuilds every figure from the raw data, compares it to the draft, and flags mismatches and large month-over-month moves. Uses its own instructions, separate from the analyst.

4 · Finance

Reviews and signs off

Approves the release, or returns it with the mismatch list. Nothing goes to management without this step.

↓ Checker flags the mismatch and sends it back once
Retry passes

Analyst corrects to 40%. Checker confirms. Draft goes to finance for sign-off.

Retry fails

Draft is held with the mismatch list attached. Finance reviews the flagged figure before anything is released.

Agent output: three parts

1 · Check immediately

Gross margin

Margin is 40%, not the 42% in the draft. Confirm the cost figure before release.

2 · Key updates

Costs up 9%

Costs rose from 550 to 600 week over week. Sales held at 1,000.

3 · Watchlist

Margin trend

Margin fell from 45% to 40% in one week. If it falls again next week, escalate to finance.

The principle: the checker never trusts the analyst's numbers. It recomputes from the source, then compares. Finance keeps final approval.
